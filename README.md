# Toy Story Memory Game

A fun, interactive memory game featuring your favorite Toy Story characters! This tool is designed for **parents who use AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication)** to play with their children, facilitating shared engagement and character recognition with a personalized touch.

## Features

*   **Personalized Greeting**: Enter your child's name for a custom "Hi [Name]!" welcome and personalized voice prompts.
*   **Voice Selection**: Choose between "Mommy" (Female) and "Daddy" (Male) modes, with specific voice selection from your system's installed voices.
*   **Interactive Speech**: The game speaks to the child, asking them to find specific characters ("Andy, where's the picture of Woody?").
*   **Find Match Challenge**: A special "Find Match" button challenges the child to find another picture of the current character.
*   **Toy Story Theme**: Styled with Andy's Room cloud wallpaper and bright, fun colors.

## How to Play

1.  **Open the Game**: Double-click `toystorymatch.html` to open it in your web browser.
2.  **Welcome Screen**:
    *   Enter the **Child's Name**.
    *   Select a preferred **Voice** for Mommy and Daddy from the dropdowns (English voices recommended).
    *   Click **Mommy** or **Daddy** to start the game.
3.  **Play**:
    *   Click **PICK CARD** (or **NEXT**) to reveal a new character.
    *   The game will ask the child to find that character.
    *   Click **Find Match** to hear a bonus question: "Can you find another picture of [Character]?"

## Requirements

*   A modern web browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Safari).
*   Sound enabled for speech synthesis.
